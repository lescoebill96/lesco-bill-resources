# How a LESCO Bill Estimate Works

An electricity bill is not based only on the number of units consumed. The final amount can also include different charges, taxes, and adjustments.

This simple resource explains the basic factors that can affect an estimated LESCO bill.

## Main factors

- **Electricity consumption:** The number of units used during the billing period.
- **Tariff:** The applicable electricity rate depends on the consumer category and tariff slab.
- **Fixed charges:** Some connections may have fixed or other applicable charges.
- **Taxes and duties:** Applicable government taxes can increase the final bill amount.
- **Adjustments:** Certain billing adjustments may also appear on an electricity bill.

Because electricity tariffs and applicable charges can change, an online estimate should be treated as a general calculation rather than an exact official bill.

For checking an actual LESCO bill, consumers can use their reference number through the relevant online bill service.
