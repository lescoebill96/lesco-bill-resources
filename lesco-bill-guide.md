# LESCO Bill Guide

This guide provides basic information for LESCO electricity consumers in Pakistan.

## How to Check a LESCO Bill

LESCO consumers can check their electricity bill online using their reference number.

For a simple step-by-step guide, visit:

[LESCO Bill Check Online](https://lescoebill.org/)

## LESCO Reference Number

The reference number is commonly used to identify an electricity connection when checking a LESCO bill online.

## Understanding Your Bill

A LESCO bill may include electricity charges, taxes, adjustments, and other applicable charges.
